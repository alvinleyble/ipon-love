# Combined view shows shared spending, not partner account balances

**Context.** Two earlier decisions interact in the combined view. Redacting partner views (ADR-0005) hide a partner's *private* transactions from the other partner, and account balance is derived from the ledger (ADR-0007). Therefore one partner cannot correctly compute the other's per-account balance — any figure shown would silently omit private activity. Separately, partner account/category names are exposed (needed to render shared transactions), which leaks the existence of accounts even if all their transactions are private.

**Decision.** The combined view shows the merged non-private transaction stream, color-coded per partner, plus shared budgets and each partner's **monthly spending total** (the sum of their visible transactions — computable and correct). It does **not** show partner per-account balances; balances remain an individual-view concept over one's own accounts. Account/category metadata (names, icons, colors) is accepted as shared-by-design, consistent with the PRD's "financially open couple" target user.

**Consequences.** Sidesteps the uncomputable-balance problem and matches the mockup's Couple View (combined stream + per-partner monthly chips), with no new screens. A partner's monthly-spending chip reflects only non-private activity — "private" genuinely means hidden, including from period totals. **Rejected:** showing partner balances anyway (silently wrong with private activity) and adding a "private account" concept now to plug the name-existence leak (deferred — a possible post-V1 "private account" feature; out of V1 scope).
