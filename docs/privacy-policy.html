Love, Ipon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026  ·  Developer: Alvin Leyble

Your financial data belongs to you. Love, Ipon does not sell your data, show you ads, or share your information with third parties beyond what is needed to run the app.

This policy explains what data Love, Ipon collects, how it is used, and how you can control it. By using the app you agree to these terms.

What we collect

Love, Ipon collects only the data you enter to use the app:

  • Account information — your email address and display name, used to create and identify your account.
  • Financial data — transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, recurring rules, and budget alerts you create inside the app.
  • Notes and attachments — rich-text notes and any images you attach to notes or transactions.
  • Couple data — if you pair with a partner, a shared couple record and any data you both mark as shared.
  • App preferences — your chosen theme, PIN lock setting, and navigation layout. These are stored locally on your device only and never sent to the cloud.

We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs, or any data unrelated to the app's finance features.

How we use your data

  • To sync your data across devices when you sign in to the same account.
  • To show your partner their permitted view of shared data — only when you have paired, and only data you choose to share.
  • To send budget alert notifications when you approach or exceed a budget limit you set.

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Supabase, a cloud database provider, in the Asia Pacific (Singapore) region. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

App preferences (theme, PIN, navigation pins) are stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore and are never sent to the cloud.

Partner sharing

Love, Ipon includes a couples pairing feature. When you pair with a partner:

  • You choose what to share — notes and accounts must be explicitly marked as shared.
  • Transactions marked as private are never visible to your partner.
  • If you unpair, shared data is removed from your partner's device on their next sync.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Love, Ipon is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
